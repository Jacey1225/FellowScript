## Executive Summary

None of the four comparables combine FellowScript's specific bundle — group-priced subscriptions,
a power-user multi-panel Reader, and a built-in social/sharing layer — and nothing found in this
pass contradicts that. But the four split into two very different competitive tiers that matter
for strategy:

**Tier 1 — mass-market scale, not real pricing/positioning comparables.** YouVersion (1B+ installs,
free, ad-free) and Hallow (~10-11M downloads, celebrity/parish-driven) operate at a scale and on a
distribution model (free habit-loop, or free-to-paid mass conversion) that a small team cannot and
should not try to out-compete head-on. They're gravity wells, not rivals to win share from
directly.

**Tier 2 — actual pricing/positioning comparables.** Abide (20M+ downloads, $9.99/mo individual
subscription) and SmartGroups (free single-group, **now-confirmed $59/mo church tier for 2+
groups**) are the two worth tracking closely. Neither prices per-member the way FellowScript does;
SmartGroups' church tier is closest in intent (group/organization-level pricing) but structured as
a flat multi-group fee rather than FellowScript's per-member-count model.

**Biggest confirmed advantage:** the group-priced-subscription-as-growth-lever mechanic is still
unmatched by any of the four. **Biggest confirmed threat:** SmartGroups is the only one already
doing AI-generated group Bible study content *and* already has a live paid organizational tier —
it is the closest thing to a direct feature-and-model competitor of the four, more than the
overview previously had pricing evidence for.

**Top 3 actions:**
1. Don't reposition against YouVersion/Hallow on distribution or price — they're not beatable on
   those axes and aren't really pricing comparables.
2. Watch SmartGroups specifically; get real pricing/feature parity data on its church tier
   (branding, pastor dashboard, priority support) since it's the nearest thing to a direct
   competitor found.
3. Category market-size data is now partially available (see below) but disagrees across
   vendors — don't put a specific TAM number in a client-facing deck without checking the
   underlying report methodology first.

## Competitor Profiles

### Hallow
- **Category:** Aspirational / mass-market (Catholic-leaning prayer & meditation)
- **Scale:** ~10-11M downloads total; ~220K downloads in the most recent 30-day window found;
  case-study cite of 2M installs driving a #1 App Store ranking. Rating 4.68/5 across ~120K
  ratings.
- **Model:** Celebrity-backed, parish-partnership-driven distribution — not primarily
  paid-acquisition or group-referral.
- **Relevance to FellowScript:** Low as a direct pricing/feature comparable; useful only as a
  distribution-tactic reference (parish partnerships), which prior `/research` already evaluated
  and deferred.

### YouVersion
- **Category:** Aspirational / category-defining mass-market
- **Scale:** 1 billion+ device installs (Nov 2025 milestone); 10-12M new installs/month; ~66K
  concurrent app-opens/second; 80%+ of downloads from outside the US (India, Africa, Latin America
  prominent).
- **Model:** Entirely free, ad-free, SEO/habit-loop distribution. No subscription tier at all —
  not a monetization comparable.
- **Relevance to FellowScript:** Confirms the "not really a comparable, more a gravity well"
  framing already in the overview. Its global/solo-habit-reading skew also confirms FellowScript's
  small-group/leader-first niche is a different market segment, not a smaller slice of the same
  one.

### Abide
- **Category:** Direct-ish (closest pricing/positioning comparable per prior overview)
- **Scale:** 20M+ downloads.
- **Pricing:** Premium $9.99/month or $39.99/year (cheapest of the scaled competitors found;
  bundles family sharing by default). Free tier gives a "limited but meaningful" selection.
- **Model:** Individual-subscriber Scripture-guided meditation/sleep-story/devotional content —
  no group-pricing mechanic, no visible group/social layer comparable to FellowScript's.
- **Relevance to FellowScript:** Confirms Abide is priced for individuals, not groups — its
  pricing comparability to FellowScript is on absolute dollar terms only, not on model. Its actual
  growth/acquisition tactics remain undocumented in what's publicly findable, as the prior
  overview already noted; this pass did not close that gap either.

### SmartGroups
- **Category:** Direct (closest feature comparable — AI + group study)
- **Scale:** Not publicly disclosed (no download/user figures found).
- **Pricing — newly confirmed this pass:** Free forever for one group with unlimited members
  (ad-free). Church/multi-group upgrade **starts at $59/month** for 2+ groups, adding custom
  branding, a pastor's dashboard, and priority support. Pricing is flat per organizational tier,
  not per-member — a structurally different model from FellowScript's per-member group pricing.
- **Model:** Zero-prep AI-generated Bible study lessons (topic or passage → full lesson +
  icebreakers), 25+ languages, attendance/prayer/group-health tracking.
- **Relevance to FellowScript:** The nearest real competitor of the four on both feature surface
  (AI + group-first) and now, pricing model (organization-tier subscription). Previously the
  overview only knew it ran "a free single-group tier + a 90-day pilot" with no further pricing
  detail — that's now filled in.

FellowScript remains the only one of the four priced explicitly *by group size as a growth lever*

nothing to contradict that as a genuine differentiator.

## Category Market Size — sourced this pass, treat cautiously

No primary data exists (no first-party FellowScript analytics, no company-disclosed TAM/SAM for
any of the four competitors). What's available comes from market-research-vendor report listings,
which disagree with each other and don't publish visible methodology:

- Bible Study Software market: ~$1.2B (2024) → ~$2.5B by 2033 (8.9% CAGR) — one vendor.
- Bible Software market (same category, different vendor): ~$1.42B (2026) → ~$10.55B by 2035
  (8.9% CAGR) — a materially different trajectory for a similarly-named category from a different
  source.
- Broader Spiritual Wellness Apps market: ~$2.52B (2025) → ~$2.89B (2026) → ~$9.91B by 2035
  (14.66% CAGR).
- Religious Studies App market: 15.10% CAGR (2025-2033); North America ~$1.7B share (2025).

**Read on this:** the category is real, growing at a real double-digit clip by every estimate
found — but the absolute dollar figures vary enough between vendors (nearly 4x difference in 2035
projections for what are nominally the same category) that none of these should go into a
client-facing number without pulling the underlying report and checking its methodology first.
This is provided as directional confirmation the market is growing, not as a defensible TAM/SAM
figure.

## SWOT — FellowScript (aggregate, informed by this pass)

**Strengths**
- Only comparable with per-member group pricing tied directly to a group/social product layer
- Power-user dockable Reader has no equivalent found among the four
- Niche is structurally distinct from the two mass-market apps (YouVersion, Hallow) — not
  competing for the same growth loop

**Weaknesses**
- Zero first-party audience/analytics data, still — every one of the four competitors' own scale
  figures are now better documented than FellowScript's own
- FellowScript's live homepage could not be independently re-verified this pass (403 on fetch);
  messaging claims rest on local docs only

**Opportunities**
- SmartGroups' AI-lesson-generation feature is the one capability among the four that maps onto
  FellowScript's own "AI Agent daily heartbeat" feature — a direct feature comparison FellowScript
  hasn't published anywhere; worth a dedicated `/market copy` or comparison-page pass later
- None of the four publish a comparison/alternatives page targeting each other — an uncontested
  content opportunity if FellowScript wanted a "vs SmartGroups" page

**Threats**
- SmartGroups is closer to a direct competitor on both features and pricing model than the prior
  overview had evidence for — worth active monitoring, not just a comparables list entry
- Category market-size growth (whichever vendor's number) means more entrants are plausible over
  the same horizon FellowScript is building for
