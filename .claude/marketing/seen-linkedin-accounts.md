# Seen LinkedIn accounts

Running log of LinkedIn account/profile names already surfaced in a past
daily marketing report's engagement queue (Step 1 social listening). Tracked
by account, not by individual post — once an account has been queued, later
posts from that same account are skipped rather than re-surfacing the same
person repeatedly. The daily pipeline checks this file before queuing a new
candidate and appends any newly queued account here after each run.

One account name/handle per line, oldest first. No other content below this line.
