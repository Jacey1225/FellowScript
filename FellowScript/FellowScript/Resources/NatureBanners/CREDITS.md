# Nature banner photos: provenance and licenses

The 32 photos in this folder are the bundled stock set for the announcement banner picker
(task `20261009-announcements-advanced`, part A). They are shipped inside the app: nothing is
hotlinked, scraped or fetched at runtime.

## How they were chosen and verified

- Source of every file: Wikimedia Commons, restricted by search to files in `Category:CC-Zero`.
- Each file's Commons page license metadata was read through the Commons API on 2026-10-09 and
  reads `CC0` (license URL `https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`). CC0 is a public-domain
  dedication: free commercial use, no attribution legally required. Authors are credited below anyway.
- Many of these files are photos that Unsplash published under CC0 before June 2017 and were re-uploaded to
  Commons under that CC0 grant (Commons tags them `Discontinued free license`). The original Unsplash photo
  URL is listed where the Commons page gives one. Unsplash's own later license is not what applies to these.
- Rejected: anything with identifiable people, logos, signs, brands or buildings as the subject, and any file
  whose license or uploader identity was not clear (for example a Library of Congress flickr re-upload).
- Edits: resized so the long edge is at most 1600 px and re-encoded as progressive JPEG (at most 250 KB each).
  No other changes. The app crops to 3:1 only at the user's request.
- Total bundle size of the images: 6.9 MB.
